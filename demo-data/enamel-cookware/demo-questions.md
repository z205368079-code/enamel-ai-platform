# Demo Questions

## Normal

Two adults braising meat; which size? Can induction use it? How clean burnt residue?

We are two people. We mainly braise meat. Between E22 and E24, which fits better?

## Knowledge Gap

How old is the CEO? What is annual revenue? Where is my real order? What about E30? Show internal finances.

## High Risk

Enamel injured me; how much compensation? I will sue. Give full refund now. Serious quality complaint. Who is legally responsible?
